Signature Dining

Naivedyam & Dassara

Naivedyam

Pure Vegetarian Restaurant

Traditional vegetarian cuisine prepared with authentic recipes and premium ingredients.

  • Kamma Neyyi Podi Dosa — Crispy golden rice-crepe layered with clarified butter and hand-pounded lentil spice mix.
  • Avakaya Annam — Royal basmati short grains infused with heritage mango pickling paste.
  • Pesarattu Upma — Traditional Andhra green gram crepe served with flavorful upma.
  • Gongura Pappu — Tangy sorrel leaves blended with slow-cooked lentils.

Dassara

Flavours of Masala

Authentic non-vegetarian cuisine with rich spices, premium ingredients, and traditional Andhra flavours.

  • Miriyala Kodi Vepudu — Succulent chicken cubes wok-tossed with crushed black peppercorns and curry leaves.
  • Gongura Mamsam Biryani — Layered basmati rice cooked with slow-braised lamb and tangy sorrel leaves.
  • Natukodi Pulusu — Traditional country chicken curry cooked with aromatic spices.
  • Royyala Iguru — Andhra-style spicy prawn delicacy.
